PLAYER ONE

A / D to move · W to jump · S to crouch · Hold F to block.
J light · K heavy · L special · U guard break · I throw · O ultimate.
H unleashes your country’s signature power. Let it recharge between uses.
Move close before striking. J → J → K launches; follow with L.

PLAYER TWO & TOUCH

Arrow keys to move, jump, and crouch · Right Ctrl to block.
Numpad 1–6 for light, heavy, special, break, throw, and ultimate.
Numpad 7 unleashes Player Two’s signature power.
On-screen controls let you play with touch. Ultimate needs a full meter.

Play solo against the computer or share your keyboard with a friend. Headphones recommended.
